# gate-job-exceptions.txt — gate steps that are deliberately NOT reached on push.
#
# Format:   <workflow>:<job>:<step-number><two or more spaces><reason>
#
# The step number is required, not optional. A row that named only the job
# excused every gate step that job had -- and every one that landed in it
# afterwards, which nobody decided: a gate step moved into a dispatch-only job,
# after that job's checkout, was accepted by all three meta-checks at once.
# Numbers go stale loudly (the row then excuses a step needing no excuse, or
# leaves one unexcused), and that is the point.
#
# workflow-step-order.py requires a row for every gate step whose job or step
# condition cannot be shown to hold on push. Same discipline as
# gate-wiring-exceptions.txt: an empty reason fails, a row naming a job that no
# longer exists fails, and a row that excuses something needing no excuse fails.
# The last one is what makes these rows disappear when the condition does.
